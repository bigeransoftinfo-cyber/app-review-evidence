What this app is

A community for adults with disabilities: friendship, dating and events. As disclosed in Resolution Center, development started from a licensed generic dating-app template. It has been rebuilt: about 45 of its 100 screens were built by us, our own Laravel backend has 286 API routes, it supports 21 languages, and all template identifiers and vendor components were removed. BigeranSoft owns the concept, brand, content and backend and submits the app directly (4.2.6).

  • Each member controls who sees their disability: nobody, matches only, or everyone.
  • Accessibility wizard at first launch, easy-read mode, text size, high contrast, and 1,100+ VoiceOver labels.
  • Live captions in calls and voice-message transcription for Deaf users.
  • Calls with volunteer sign-language interpreters (TİD, ASL, BSL, DGS, LSF, ISL).
  • A private energy diary for chronic illness, with a “taking a break” status.
  • Safe-meeting tools: trusted contact, check-in timer, safety guide; plus a hidden-words harassment filter.
  • Accessible places rated by members, and disability helplines by country.
