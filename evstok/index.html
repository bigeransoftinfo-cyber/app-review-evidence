What this app is

A fully offline household pantry and expiry-date tracker. There is no server and no account; all data stays in on-device SQLite. Written by BigeranSoft in Flutter (119 source files, ~30,000 lines).

  • Tracks food and household supplies by storage place: fridge, freezer, pantry shelf, cabinet.
  • Warns before items expire, and gives storage-specific freshness rules (for example, freezer only).
  • Predicts when staples will run out from the household's own consumption.
  • Builds the shopping list automatically and moves purchases into stock.
  • Adds items quickly by barcode, by reading a receipt photo, or by voice.
  • Not a variant of our Stokxpro: that is a server-based business app for shops (POS, invoices, customer accounts, staff). The only file the two projects share is a debug-only test helper that is inactive in release builds.

How to verify in a few minutes

Finish the short setup, add an item by barcode or receipt, then open the expiry list and the shopping list.
